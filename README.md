📊 Key Highlights & Metrics

Total Customers: 3,900

Average Order Value: $59.76

Average Review Rating: 3.75 / 5.0

Subscription Rate: 27% Subscribers vs. 73% Non-Subscribers

Customer Retention: 97.87% Repeat Buyers

📈 Power BI Dashboard Overview

Page 1 (Overview & Revenue): Visualizes overall sales performance, customer demographic splits (age group & gender), top product categories, and subscription distribution.

Page 2 (Retention & Channels): Focuses on repeat buyer behavior, payment method popularity, shipping tier preference, and discount impact on ratings.
